PICTURE 1 of 4 - "Abandoned soon after birth, you were raised by the Naia."
Night inside an ancient forest. Between the huge mossy roots of one enormous old tree (trunk rising out of the top of the frame) rests a small woven wicker basket lined with a cream cloth; only a tiny newborn hand peeks out (no face). Tucked beside the cloth: a small old leather-bound book with a worn clasp.
The Naia have found the baby and gather around protectively: AQUPONG leans over the basket rim peering in curiously; HWIRY is curled up against the basket keeping it warm with its fluffy fins; BULCOI sits a little to one side and its flame tufts give the scene a warm firelight; BOLTO floats just above the basket on its storm cloud like a little canopy; PURUNA on its seed bulb and TOGRA peek out from between the roots. Their soft glows and Bulcoi's flames light the basket; the rest of the forest falls into deep teal shadow.
Basket slightly left of centre at about 55% height. Tender, hushed, protective, a little bit cute.
