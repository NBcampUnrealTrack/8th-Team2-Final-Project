PICTURE 2 of 4 - "You grew up in a world of spirits, with no other person in it."
Same spirit forest, now a soft morning with dappled green-gold light through the canopy. A wide mossy clearing with a small clear stream, giant roots, ferns and pale glowing flowers. A small child (about five years old) with short tousled hair and a plain linen tunic, seen from behind / three-quarter back so the face is hidden, barefoot, laughing pose with arms out, running after a playful group of spirit wisps that loop around them. More wisps rest on roots and float over the stream like friends. Absolutely no other people, no buildings: an untouched spirit-only world.
Child small in frame (about 1/6 of frame height), placed at the left third; the clearing opens toward the right. Joyful, free, bright but still muted storybook palette.
