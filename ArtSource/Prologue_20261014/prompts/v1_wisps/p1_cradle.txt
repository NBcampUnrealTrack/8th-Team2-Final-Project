PICTURE 1 of 4 - "Abandoned, and found by spirits."
Night / deep blue hour inside an ancient spirit forest. Between the huge mossy roots of one enormous old tree (trunk rising out of the top of the frame) rests a small woven wicker basket lined with a cream cloth. Only a tiny newborn hand peeks out of the cloth (no face). Tucked beside the cloth: a small old leather-bound book with a worn clasp (it will matter later).
Around the basket about a dozen spirit wisps gather curiously at different heights, the closest ones leaning in. Their light is the only light source: it warms the basket and the roots, while the rest of the forest falls into deep teal shadow with a few far, dim wisps between the trunks. Tiny floating light motes. Basket placed slightly left of centre, at about 55% height.
Tender, hushed, protective feeling.
