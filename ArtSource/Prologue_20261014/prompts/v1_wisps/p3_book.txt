PICTURE 3 of 4 - "You even learned the language of humans."
Same spirit forest at golden late afternoon. On a broad moss-covered root sits an older child (about ten, same short tousled hair, same plain linen clothes, now with a simple woven sash), seen from the side-back so the face is hidden by hair. On their lap lies the same small old leather-bound book that was in the basket, opened. Several spirit wisps hover close above the pages like reading lamps, their glow lighting the pages and the child's hands; one wisp sits on the child's shoulder. The pages show only illegible tiny marks and a simple drawing of a house, NOT readable text.
Beside the root: the faded cream cloth from the basket, now used as a little bundle. Warm, quiet, curious, a sense of time passing. Child placed slightly right of centre.
