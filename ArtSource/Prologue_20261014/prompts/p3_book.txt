PICTURE 3 of 4 - "You even learned the language of humans."
Same forest at golden late afternoon. On a broad moss-covered root sits an older child (about ten, same short pale-blond tousled hair, linen clothes with a simple woven sash), seen from the side-back so the face is hidden by hair. On their lap lies the same small old leather-bound book from the basket, opened; its pages show only tiny illegible marks and a simple drawing of a house (NOT readable text).
The Naia gather to "read" together: BULCOI sits close and its flame tufts light the pages like a reading lamp; HWIRY is curled on the child's shoulder; PURUNA leans in from the other side staring at the pages with its big eye; AQUPONG and TOGRA sit side by side in front, listening; BOLTO dozes on its cloud above. Beside the root: the faded cream cloth from the basket tied into a little bundle.
Child slightly right of centre. Warm, quiet, curious, a sense of time passing.
