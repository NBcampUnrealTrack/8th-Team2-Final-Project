PROJECT: opening prologue of "Naia Unbounds", a cozy fantasy creature-companion RPG rendered in HD-2D pixel art (pixel sprites in a softly lit diorama, like Octopath Traveler / Sea of Stars). The prologue is a 4-picture "pixel storybook" told in second person: an abandoned newborn is raised by spirit creatures called Naia in a forest where no humans live, grows up with them, and finally walks out into the human world.

THE NAIA (most important): the spirits ARE the game's real Naia creatures shown in the attached creature sheet. Draw them faithfully - same body shapes, colours, markings and cute chibi proportions - as living characters with personality, not as abstract lights. Give each a very soft faint glow at its outline so they read as spirits, but keep their designs fully clear and recognisable. Size: each Naia is about the size of a cat or small dog next to a human child.
- AQUPONG: round white/pale-blue seal-like blob, a glossy deep-blue water-droplet cap on its head, big dark eyes, tiny pink heart nose, small light-blue fin tail.
- BOLTO: round pale-yellow kitten face with big dark eyes and small ears marked with blue spark crosses, always sitting inside a swirling blue-grey storm cloud that it floats on.
- PURUNA: lime-green leafy little creature with two small horns, ONE big round yellow eye, a white fluffy collar and white leaf-like arms, sitting on a dark-green striped seed bulb.
- BULCOI: chunky orange boar-like creature with black fur on top, grey stone horns/plates, small orange flame tufts at its head and tail, one yellow eye.
- TOGRA: dark-grey mole/seal-like lump with a brown speckled patch on its back, pink snout, small yellow eye.
- HWIRY: pale-pink fluffy cat-like creature with a blue eye, a pointed ear, wispy fish-like fins and a long tail ending in a fin.

STYLE (identical for every picture of the set):
- High quality hand-crafted PIXEL ART illustration. Clean, consistent square pixels as if drawn on a 704x396 canvas and enlarged with nearest-neighbour; NO blurry gradients, NO painterly brush smears, NO photo texture, NO 3D render look.
- Soft HD-2D lighting: light sources get a gentle pixel bloom halo, cool shadows, warm highlights. A few tiny floating light motes in the air.
- Storybook memory mood: gentle vignette toward the corners, tender, warm, playful, never scary or grim.
- Human figures are always seen from behind or side-back, faces never visible, gender-ambiguous, simple undyed linen clothes, short tousled pale-blond hair.
- Canvas: landscape. Compose for a 16:9 frame; keep all important content inside the central 16:9 band. Keep the lowest ~20% of the frame calm and darker because white subtitle text will sit there.
- STRICTLY NO text, letters, numbers, logos, signatures, watermarks, borders, frames, UI, speech bubbles.
