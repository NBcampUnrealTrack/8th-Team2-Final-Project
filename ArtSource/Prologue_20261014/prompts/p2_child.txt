PICTURE 2 of 4 - "You grew up in the world of the Naia, with no other person in it."
Same forest on a bright morning, dappled green-gold light through the canopy. A wide mossy clearing with a small clear stream, giant roots, ferns and pale flowers. A small child (about five years old, short pale-blond tousled hair, plain linen tunic, barefoot), seen from behind / three-quarter back so the face is hidden, runs laughing with arms out, playing tag with the Naia: BOLTO zips ahead on its storm cloud, AQUPONG splashes and plays in the stream, PURUNA hops along on its seed bulb, HWIRY glides beside the child with its fins spread, TOGRA pops out of a burrow hole in the grass, BULCOI trots behind with its little flames flickering. No other people, no buildings: an untouched world of Naia.
Child small in frame (about 1/6 of frame height), placed at the left third; the clearing opens toward the right. Joyful, free, bright but still storybook-soft.
